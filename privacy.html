Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Relay ("Relay", "we", "us") is an app that makes phone calls on your behalf using an automated AI assistant — for example, booking a restaurant abroad or getting a quote from a local business. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it in plain English.

Who is responsible for your data (the "data controller"):
[Your Company] Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (company number [company number]), registered office [registered office address].
Contact: support@yourdomain.com

1. The short version

  • We collect the account and call details we need to place calls for you.
  • Every call opens by clearly disclosing that it is an AI assistant calling on your behalf.
  • Calls are recorded to run and improve the service; recordings are automatically deleted after 7 days. Written transcripts are kept until you delete them.
  • We use trusted service providers (listed below) to place calls and run the app. Some are outside the UK/EU.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.
  • You can delete your account — and everything in it — at any time from inside the app.

3. The person you call, and call recording

When Relay places a call, the assistant's first words disclose that it is an automated AI assistant calling on behalf of a customer, and that the call may be recorded. If the person does not wish to speak with an AI, the assistant apologises and ends the call. We record calls to deliver the result to you, to auto-refund a credit if a call fails, and to keep the service safe and accurate. Call recordings are deleted automatically 7 days after the call. The written transcript and outcome are kept until you delete the call or your account.

4. Why we're allowed to use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service you asked for (placing calls, managing credits, showing results) — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the service secure, reliable and lawful, and to improve it — our legitimate interests, balanced against your rights.
  • Where required by law — e.g. keeping basic transaction records.

6. How long we keep it

  • Call recordings: deleted automatically 7 days after the call.
  • Transcripts, translations and outcomes: kept until you delete the call or your account.
  • Account & call-request data: kept while your account is active.
  • Purchase/transaction records: kept as required for tax and accounting.

7. Your rights

Under UK/EU data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete, or export your data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. You can:

  • Delete everything yourself — Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, calls, transcripts and recordings.
  • Contact us at support@yourdomain.com for any other request. We respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Relay is not intended for anyone under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
