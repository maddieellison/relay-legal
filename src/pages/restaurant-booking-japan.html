Calling in Japan

Book a restaurant in Japan without speaking Japanese

Relay can call a Japanese restaurant in Japanese and request a reservation on your behalf. You provide the restaurant number and booking details; Relay handles the conversation and returns the available outcome.

{{DOWNLOAD}}

Prepare the details first

Include the date, preferred time, party size and name for the reservation. Add a time range if you can be flexible, and review the plan before Relay starts the call.

Relay identifies itself as an AI assistant. Calls are placed only between 09:00 and 20:00 in the destination’s local time.

Use a Japanese number

The phone number must match Japan as the selected destination. Relay assigns Japanese from the number’s location rather than offering a language picker.

The restaurant controls the outcome

Relay can request a table, but a booking depends on the restaurant answering and agreeing. Check the returned date, time, party size, name and any conditions; confirm critical details directly with the restaurant.
