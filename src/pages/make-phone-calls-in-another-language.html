Supported-language calling

Make phone calls in another language

Relay can call a business and speak the language assigned to its supported destination. This can help when travelling or when a reservation, appointment, quote or information request needs a local-language phone call.

How Relay chooses the language

The iPhone app detects the country from the business number and tells you which language Relay will speak. The language is fixed by country; you cannot select a different one for the call.

Relay currently uses English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Italian and European Portuguese across its offered destinations.

Important regional limits

Belgium uses French and Switzerland uses German. Spanish-speaking destinations use one Spain Spanish voice, and Portuguese calls use European Portuguese.

What language support means

Relay handles the supported call language, but it cannot guarantee that a business will answer or agree to a request. The business number must match the chosen country, and Relay returns the available outcome and details after the call.
