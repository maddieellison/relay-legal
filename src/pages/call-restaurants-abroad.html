Restaurants while travelling

Call restaurants abroad

Relay can call a restaurant abroad on your behalf and speak the supported language assigned to that destination. Ask it to check availability or request a reservation, then review the available outcome in the iPhone app.

{{DOWNLOAD}}

What to include in your request

Give Relay the restaurant’s number and the details the restaurant will need: your preferred date and time, party size, name and any useful flexibility.

The number must match the destination you choose. Relay shows the call language detected from that number’s location; there is no manual language picker.

Before Relay calls

Review the plan carefully. Relay calls during the destination’s local calling hours and introduces itself as an AI assistant acting for you.

A request, not a guaranteed booking

A restaurant must answer and agree before a reservation can be made. It may be full, decline AI-assisted calls or ask for information Relay does not have; Relay returns the available outcome so you can decide what to do next.
