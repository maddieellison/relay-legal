What can Relay call about?

Relay can ask a restaurant about availability or request a reservation, call a business for an appointment, request a quote, or ask for practical information such as opening hours.

For travel, Relay can speak the supported language assigned to the destination, so you do not need to handle the conversation yourself.

What happens on the call

Relay identifies itself as an AI assistant calling on your behalf. A real person or business still decides whether they can help.

What Relay cannot promise

Relay cannot guarantee that a business will answer, offer availability, accept a booking or provide a quote. Review important details in the outcome and confirm anything critical directly with the business.
