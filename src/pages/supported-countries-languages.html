Current release-candidate coverage

Relay supported countries and languages

The current Relay iPhone release candidate offers calls to business numbers in 18 destinations and uses seven spoken languages. The destination is detected from the phone number and determines the call language; there is no manual language picker.

Calling limits

The business number must match the selected destination. Calls are placed only between 09:00 and 20:00 in the destination’s local time.

Emergency and crisis numbers, premium-rate and shared-cost numbers, unallocated ranges and freephone numbers are refused. Austria’s 0800 range is the freephone exception.

Availability can change

This list reflects the current TestFlight release candidate, not a live App Store release. A call still depends on rollout availability, the business answering and the business agreeing to help.
