Product

What is Relay?

Relay is a consumer iPhone app for handing off everyday phone calls to an AI assistant.

What does Relay do?

A user tells Relay what they need. Relay calls the real business, handles the conversation and reports back with the available outcome and details.

Who is Relay for?

Relay is for people who want help with everyday phone admin, including travellers who need to call a business in a supported destination and language.

What kinds of calls can Relay make?

Relay can make requests for restaurant reservations, appointments and quotes, or ask for information such as availability and opening hours.

How Relay works

How does Relay work?

Tell Relay what you need, review the call plan and let Relay make the call. The business receives a normal phone call; it does not need Relay installed.

Does Relay identify itself as AI?

Yes. Relay introduces itself as an AI assistant calling on the user’s behalf.

Does the business need Relay installed?

No. Relay calls the business’s phone number.

What happens after a call?

The user receives the available outcome and details in Relay. Important details should be checked directly with the business.

Coverage and availability

What destinations are supported?

The current Relay iPhone release candidate offers 18 calling destinations. The maintained destination list and country-to-language mapping are on the supported countries and languages page.

What languages are supported?

Relay currently supports seven call languages. The language is assigned from the selected destination; see the canonical coverage reference for the current mapping.

Is an outcome guaranteed?

No. Outcomes depend on the business answering and agreeing. Relay cannot guarantee a reservation, appointment, quote or other result.

Which platforms currently support Relay?

Relay currently supports iOS on iPhone.
