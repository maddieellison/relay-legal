How Relay works

Tell Relay what you need and review the call plan. Relay then calls the real business, handles the conversation as an identified AI assistant, and returns the available outcome and details to you.

From request to outcome

  1. Tell Relay what you need.Choose the business, add the details and review the plan before the call starts.
  2. Relay makes the call.The assistant calls the real business during supported local calling hours.
  3. Relay handles the conversation.It introduces itself as an AI assistant calling on your behalf and makes your request.
  4. You receive the available outcome.Review what happened and the details returned in the app, including when the answer is no.

What to expect

Relay can make requests for reservations, appointments and quotes, or ask for information such as availability and opening hours. Outcomes depend on the business answering and agreeing, so Relay cannot guarantee a booking, appointment, quote or other result.
