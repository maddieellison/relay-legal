Appointments by phone

AI calling for appointments

Relay can call a business and request an appointment on your behalf. Tell Relay the service, preferred times and relevant details, then review the available outcome in the iPhone app.

{{DOWNLOAD}}

Give Relay a clear request

Include what the appointment is for, dates and times that work, your name and any flexibility. Review the plan before Relay calls so the assistant has the information it needs.

Relay identifies itself as an AI assistant during the call. It can ask about availability and make the request, but the business remains in control of its diary.

Check the returned details

Review the business name, service, date, time and any instructions. Confirm important or sensitive arrangements directly with the provider.

Appointments are not guaranteed

The business may not answer, may have no suitable time or may require you to contact it directly. Relay returns the available result even when the request cannot be completed.
