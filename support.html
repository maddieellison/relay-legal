Common questions

How do I get a refund for a call that didn't work?

You don't need to do anything — if a call fails (no answer, hang-up, or the task couldn't be completed), the credit is refunded to your Relay balance automatically.

Does the person I'm calling know it's an AI?

Yes. Every call begins by clearly stating it's an automated AI assistant calling on your behalf, and that the call may be recorded.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, calls, transcripts and recordings.
