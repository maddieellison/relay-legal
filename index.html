Relay

The phone calls you'd rather not make — made for you, in any language.

Relay makes phone calls on your behalf using a polite AI assistant. Book a restaurant abroad, get a quote from a local trade, or sort a booking at home — you tell Relay who to call and what you need, and it makes the call and reports back. Every call opens by disclosing that it's an AI assistant calling for you.
